Q2. Marathon wale toh Road par daudte hain, toh hum kyun nahi?
Marathon aur Police 1500m mein zameen-aasmaan ka farak hai:
1. Surface Match: Marathon ki race road par hoti hai. Lekin tumhari Police Bharti Bashing ke MITTI / GROUND track par hogi. Mitti par grip aur pairon ka balance road se alag hota hai.

🌬️ KULLU COLD AIR & GALA PROTECTION (BREATHING SECRET)
Kullu ki thandi hawa se gala kharab hone se bachne ki 2 Master Techniques:
1. Jibh Taalu Par (Tongue Behind Upper Teeth): Daudte waqt jibh ki tip ko upar taalu se chipka kar rakho aur hoth halke khule rakho. Thandi hawa direct gale par nahi lagegi — warm hokar lungs mein jayegi. Gala 0% sukhega!
2. Pre-Run Drink: Ghar se nikalne se 20 min pehle 1 Cup Gunguna Paani + 1 Chammach Shehad (Honey) pi lo. Shehad gale par layer bana deta hai jisse thand ka asar nahi hota.
📅 10-DAY ACCELERATED 1500M SCHEDULE
Din Subah Ka Running Workout (Main) Shaam Ka Session (Recovery & Jumps)

Day 2 (Lungs) Track ke 4 round (1600m) continuous rhythmic jog. Breathing rhythm set karo. Light stretching + Long Jump approach run practice.
Day 3 (Intervals) 400m x 3 rounds (Har round 1:35 min target). Beech mein 2 min paidal walk rest. Plank (60 sec x 2) + Calf raises (25 reps x 2).
Day 4 (Speed) 100m ke 3 explosive sprints (Target: 13.5s) + 400m easy jog finish. High Jump & Long Jump confidence check (2-3 jumps).
Day 5 (Tempo) 800m fast pace (Target: 2 min 55 sec) + 3 min rest + 400m strong finish. Light jogging + Hamstring & Quad static stretching.
Day 6 (Endurance) 400m x 4 rounds (Har round 1:30 min target). Beech mein 90 sec rest. Upper body & core workout (Pushups + Squats).
Day 7 (REST) COMPLETE REST (NO RUNNING). Sarson tel maalish + Garam paani. Full rest. Sleep early.
Day 8 (Benchmark) 1200m (3 rounds) Fast Timing Check. Target: Under 4 min 20 sec! Walk + Jumps form review + Deep stretching.
Day 9 (Active) 2 round easy jog + 100m sprint form check + full flexibility drills. Mental focus + Kit inspection (shoes/socks).
Day 10 (TRIAL) 🔥 OFFICIAL 1500M TRIAL WITH STOPWATCH (Target: < 5:30 MIN)! Victory walk + Celebration!
