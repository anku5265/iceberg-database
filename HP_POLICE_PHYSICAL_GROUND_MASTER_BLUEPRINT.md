### 3. Phase 2: The Master Running Protocol (10-Day Accelerated Schedule)

#### 🌬️ Golden Breathing Rule (Har Round Mein Follow Karo):
* **Jibh Taalu Par (Tongue on Roof of Mouth):** Hoth halke se khule rakho aur jibh upar taalu se touch rakho — thandi hawa garm hokar andar jayegi, gala 0% sukhega.
* **Dual Breathing:** Naak + Muh dono se natural saans aane do.
* **Rhythm:** 2 kadam saans andar, 2 kadam saans bahar (`In-In, Out-Out`).

#### 📅 The 10-Day Progressive Plan:
* **Day 1 (Base Building):**  
  Track ke **3 round (1200m)** continuous slow jogging. Stop nahi hona, chahe walking se thoda tez speed ho.  
  + 3 min rest + 2x 100m Strides.
* **Day 2 (Aerobic Expansion):**  
  Track ke **4 round (1600m)** continuous rhythmic jogging. Speed control mein, lungs ko broad karo.
* **Day 3 (400m Intervals):**  
  400m x 3 rounds. Har round ka time: **1 min 35 sec**. Beech mein 2 min paidal walk/rest.
* **Day 4 (Jump & Speed Day):**  
  100m ke 3 explosive sprints (Target: ~13.5 sec) + High jump & Long jump 2-3 casual practice.
* **Day 5 (Tempo Run):**  
  2 round (800m) fast rhythm mein (Target: 2 min 55 sec) + 3 min rest + 1 round (400m) hard.
* **Day 6 (Speed Endurance):**  
  400m x 4 rounds (Har round: **1 min 30 sec**, beech mein 90 sec rest).
* **Day 7 (FULL RECOVERY DAY):**  
  Complete Rest. Sarson ke tel se pindi aur thikano ki maalish. Garam paani se nahana.
* **Day 8 (The 1200m Benchmark):**  
  3 round (1200m) fast timing check. Target: Under 4 min 20 sec!
* **Day 9 (Active Easy Jog):**  
  2 round easy jog + Full body stretching.
* **Day 10 (THE 1500M OFFICIAL TRIAL):**  
  Stopwatch ON karo. **1500m under 5:30 touch!**

### 4. Phase 3: Post-Run Strength Work (10 Mins)

1. **Bodyweight Deep Squats:** 20 reps x 2 sets (Thighs aur glutes power).
2. **Calf Raises (Kissi step/patthar par khade hokar):** 25 reps x 2 sets  
   *(Shin splints aur pindi ke dard ko 100% khatam karta hai).*
3. **Walking Lunges:** 15 kadam (Hip flexor strength).
4. **Plank:** 45 to 60 seconds x 2 sets (Core strong banata hai taaki daudte waqt body jhuke na).

### 5. Phase 4: Cool Down & Static Stretching (8 Mins)

3. **Calf Wall Stretch:** Deewar ya ped par hath rakh kar piche wala pair kheecho (Calf stretch).
4. **Butterfly Stretch:** Dono pair ke talwe jod kar zameen par baitho.
